How It Works

Our AI pipeline processes sign language in three simple steps

📷

Camera Capture

The backend opens your server-side camera and captures video frames at up to 30 fps using OpenCV.

🤚

Hand Detection

MediaPipe's HandLandmarker detects hands and extracts 21 3D landmark points, drawing a live skeleton overlay.

🧠

Sign Translation

Landmarks are preprocessed into a normalized feature vector and fed into an ML model to predict the sign being made.
